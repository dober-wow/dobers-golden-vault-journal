Port of Luskan

Ministry of Harbour & Victualling

Office of Manifests · Berth Eleven

By tide & ledger

Cook’s Warrant No. LSK–VIC–4471
Blood Feather
Galley Crew — Cook
Kenku · black plumage · unremarkable height · speaks in borrowed voices
Jolly Pelican
Eleven, Port of Luskan
Northern Supply — Revel’s End receiving station
Bethra
Ches, 1492 DR · Luskan quay

The bearer named above is engaged to the galley of the merchant vessel Jolly Pelican for the northern supply circuit, and is entitled to passage, ship’s rations, and the freedom of the working decks in the discharge of that duty.

To be produced on demand before any officer of the Watch, the Harbour Ministry, or the receiving station. Warrant void if transferred.

H. Vellory

Clerk of Manifests, by order of the Harbourmaster

Ship’s serjeant-at-arms
Ches, 1492 DR

The bearer named above is retained as relief guard aboard the merchant vessel Jolly Pelican for the northern supply circuit, to stand watch-rotation and spell the standing crew across the crossing.
