The mess-table drill — Kess

Three years on the Mintarn Roost rotation, according to this ink. The quizzing goes past the roster line: who trained you? what does the Roost smell like, come winter? Build the memories until they answer themselves.

The kenku is never anonymous: the only kenku this coast has seen, and the Varkenbluff posters sketch one. The name Kess must be standing there before the question arrives. Borrowed voices stay stowed unless the legend would use them.

The mess-table drill — Marta Greel

A clerk’s error is why he goes by Greel on the manifest — and always has. The drill works the soft spot: how does a clerk’s error survive the muster roll? Last posting? Watch rotation — dawn or dusk? Know those answers like your own hands.

If anyone reads “Marta” aloud: a tired shrug, once, never twice. Men who tell the story twice are men who rehearsed it.

The mess-table drill — Neris Fane

Varrin saves the hardest scrutiny for last — and the discipline is the opposite of hiding: hood down when you speak, gloves off, eyes up. You’re not ashamed, you’re bored. Concealment is the tell; save the hood for the wind and the watch, where every soul on deck wears one. And nothing fey happens where it can be seen. Not once.
