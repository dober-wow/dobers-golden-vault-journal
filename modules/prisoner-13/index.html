Overview

Far to the frozen north, past the reach of any friendly banner, the arctic prison of Revel's End holds the Sword Coast's most dangerous secrets behind blade-cliff walls. One of those secrets is a name: Korda Glintstone, a Zhentarim spymaster the Golden Vault needs to reach before someone quieter does.

The operatives must gain entry to the prison under cover, locate Prisoner 13, and extract what they came for — testimony, a cipher, or the prisoner themselves — before the warden's suspicion boils over into a full lockdown.

Suspicion Clock: Every misstep raises the prison's alert level. At maximum suspicion the warden orders a lockdown and the extraction window closes.

Adventure Background

Korda Glintstone spent a career trading secrets between the Zhentarim and anyone who could pay. When a deal soured, they were buried in the deepest cell Revel's End could offer — officially forgotten, unofficially still worth a fortune to the right buyer.

Key points:

  • Korda is held under a false name in the maximum-security tier.
  • The warden, Marthannis, runs the prison by suspicion and precedent.
  • A rival faction is already moving to silence Korda for good.
  • The prison's arctic isolation is its strongest wall — and the party's biggest problem on the way out.

Using the Golden Vault

Deliver a golden key to the characters. When they open the music box:

"Greetings, operatives. In the arctic prison of Revel's End sits a prisoner known only as number thirteen — a name that, spoken to the wrong ears, would cost a great many lives. We need what they know before it is silenced forever. Gain entry, reach Prisoner 13, and secure their testimony. The cold is not your ally, and neither is the warden. Move carefully, and move quickly. Good luck, operatives."

The Mark — Korda Glintstone

Korda is cautious, transactional, and always negotiating. They will not simply follow rescuers — they will price the rescue. Persuasion, leverage, or a shared enemy opens the door.

Trait Detail
Disposition Wary → cooperative if convinced of a better deal
Wants Out alive, and to know who ordered the hit on them
Leverage A cipher naming Zhentarim assets across the North

Security & Suspicion

Suspicion Levels

The prison tracks the party's cover. Each level tightens patrols and shortens the window.

Level State Effect
0–1 Routine Standard patrols; cover holds
2–3 Watchful Extra checks; DC +2 on Deception
4 Alert Cells swept; guards doubled
5 Lockdown Extraction window closes

Prison Locations

DM map — ground level, with cell tiers and patrol routes keyed

R1: Intake & Search

A blast of frigid air greets you as the iron door grinds open. Guards in fur-lined mail wave you toward a cold stone counter where every possession is logged, weighed, and taken.

Cover Check: Contraband raises suspicion. A successful Sleight of Hand or a solid forged manifest keeps gear hidden.

R7: The Maximum Tier

The corridor narrows. Here the doors have no windows, only a slot at boot height and a number stamped in iron. The thirteenth is no different from the rest — which is exactly the point.

Guards: Two wardens rotate every ten minutes. Cell 13 opens only to the warden's key or a DC 18 Thieves' Tools check.

Conclusion

Extraction Success

Korda delivers the cipher and, if extracted alive, becomes a recurring Golden Vault contact.

Rewards:

  • Zhentarim cipher (plot key for later heists)
  • Uncommon magic item of the party's choice

Extraction Failure

If suspicion hits lockdown, the party must escape into the arctic with the warden's full attention — frame it as a flight, not a firefight.

Scaling Notes (3 PCs, Level 5)

Tip: With a smaller party, favor stealth and negotiation. Direct fights against a fully-alerted prison should read as "get out," not "win the room."
